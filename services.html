Our Services

Rai Automotive provides electrical diagnostics, electrical repair, sensor calibration, and vehicle coding. We specialize in luxury European vehicles and also work with many other makes and models.

Electrical Diagnostics

Warning lights or an electrical problem you cannot figure out? We investigate the issue to identify its cause and determine the next steps.

  • Diagnosis of electrical and electronic system faults
  • Investigation of dashboard warning messages
  • Troubleshooting problems that occur intermittently

Electrical Repair

Once the problem has been identified, we carry out the electrical repairs needed to address it. Contact us to discuss your vehicle and its symptoms.

Sensor and Vehicle Calibration

We provide calibration services for supported vehicle sensors and electronic systems. Tell us your vehicle's make, model, and year so we can discuss the calibration it needs.

Vehicle Coding

We provide coding for supported vehicle electronic components, configuring them for the vehicle they are installed in. Available coding services depend on your vehicle and its equipment.

Car Parts

Need a part for your vehicle? Contact us with your car's make, model, year, and the part you are looking for to discuss availability and compatibility.

Home Diagnostic Visits

If your car will not run, a specialist can come to your home to check the vehicle. Contact us to discuss your location, the problem, and availability for a visit.

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

Describe the problem in our appointment request form, and a representative will contact you to discuss the next steps. You can also message us on Instagram for direct contact.
